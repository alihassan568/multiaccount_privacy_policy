MultiAccount Privacy Policy

Developer: Velgora Digital · Effective date: October 1, 2026

In short: MultiAccount does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no account to create. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

1. Who we are

MultiAccount ("the app") is developed by Velgora Digital ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it on your Android device.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. The app has no servers of its own, contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software, and does not transmit any data to us.

3. Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your device:

  • Web accounts: the names and addresses of the web accounts you add, plus the website data (such as cookies, saved logins and site storage) that each website stores in that account's private, isolated storage, and small website icons.
  • App clones: apps you clone are installed in a separate work profile (the "clone space") on your device. Their data is managed by those apps and by Android, not by MultiAccount.

You can delete this data at any time: sign out of or delete a web account in the app, remove the clone space in the app, or uninstall MultiAccount. Uninstalling removes the app's stored data from your device.

4. Websites and apps you use through MultiAccount

When you open a web account, your device connects directly to the website you chose, and that website receives information as it would in any web browser (for example your IP address and anything you enter). Apps you clone work the same way they do outside the clone space. Those services are operated by third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not see, receive or control that information.

5. Permissions

PermissionWhy it's used
Internet, network stateTo load the websites you add as web accounts.
Camera, microphoneOnly when a website you're using asks for them (for example a voice or video call), and only after you allow it. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere by MultiAccount.
Install packagesTo install a copy of an app you already have into the clone space when you choose to clone it. Android asks you to confirm every install.
Device administration (work profile)To create and manage the clone space using Android's built-in work profile. MultiAccount only manages the clone space and has no control over the rest of your device.
Storage (Android 9 and older only)To save files you download from a web account to your Downloads folder.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell or rent any information, because we do not collect any.

7. Security

Web account data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't access. Clones live in an Android work profile, which Android keeps separate from your personal apps.

8. Children

MultiAccount is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
